Today

What I enjoy is making complexity understandable and usable: shaping systems of information and knowledge until they stop resisting the people reaching into them.

chapter one · sound

That instinct started in an unlikely place. I studied electroacoustics and computer music, and spent years configuring unwieldy technology into something a performer could play fluently. The material has changed since then, but the work is recognizably the same. Whether it is a taxonomy, a help center, a governed prompt library, or an employer brand, I am structuring knowledge so the right person finds the right thing at the moment they need it. Increasingly, so the right AI does too.

That decade of sound was real work in real rooms: audio for dance, film, and theatre at venues including the Musée des beaux-arts de Montréal, Judson Church, and The Kitchen.

Madness: the Podcast, co-producer, featured on Michael Moore's Rumble

chapter two · shopify

The systems instinct met scale at Shopify. I joined the Employer Brand team as an intern in July 2021 and was promoted to manager in about five months. I was a pivotal contributor to the Employer Value Proposition from kickoff to launch, working with C-suite stakeholders, wrote paid campaign copy across EMEA, APAC, and the Americas, and grew the employer brand LinkedIn following 76%.

chapter three · queertech

At QueerTech, I produced the QT Podcast: 30 plus guests including Lightspeed's CEO, with Canada Media Fund, Google Chrome, and RBC as sponsors. The show served a network of 8,000+ 2SLGBTQ+ tech professionals; social engagement grew 50% during my tenure, and a 6-person media crew I directed at the Qonference returned 4x ROI on marketing spend. The community's growth was a team effort within a small team, and I say that plainly.

chapter four · systems

At Premier Health of America, I built the content systems layer for a seven-brand healthcare staffing platform. An 87-prompt governed library with review gates. A cascading brand voice system that compressed content production from over four weeks to five days. A 270-term bilingual taxonomy grounded in scheduler research. A knowledge base access model with five sensitivity tiers, designed so exclusion is explicit governance rather than an accident. A findability program treating Google, AI assistants, and on-site search as one problem; the company was acquired before rollout completed, so I present its KPIs as the measurement system I designed, not as achieved results.

chapter five · now

I hold a quiet conviction underneath all of it: real conversations, art, and lived experience are what deserve human effort. Systems are not the destination. They are what we build so attention can go where it belongs. A knowledge base is good when nobody has to think about it, because the person who needed the answer is already back in the conversation that mattered.

That conviction now has a name. Certainty Craft is the practice where this systems work continues. Visit the practice

Based in Montreal. Open to roles in content systems, AI content operations, knowledge management, and information architecture. English-language, remote across Canada or Montreal hybrid. Non-binary (they/them).
