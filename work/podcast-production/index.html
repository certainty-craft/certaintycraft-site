Context

QueerTech is a non-profit serving 8,000+ 2SLGBTQ+ tech professionals across Canada. The organization wanted a flagship content vehicle that could attract sponsors, build thought leadership, and give senior tech leaders a reason to engage with the community.

The problem, precisely

Podcasts fail in two places: they never ship, or they ship but nobody listens. The challenge was building a production system rigorous enough to land major sponsors and senior guests, while staying lean enough for a non-profit budget and a small team.

What I built

End-to-end production: preproduction research and guest outreach, vocal coaching for hosts, intro and outro craft, music procurement, in-studio and on-site recording, script review, and season strategy. Sponsor relationships: Canada Media Fund, Google Chrome, and RBC, won with a pitch that positioned the show as reaching an underserved professional audience with influence, not as a charity case. Guest booking at scale: 30+ tech leaders across early career to C-suite, culminating in an interview with Dax Dasilva, CEO and Founder of Lightspeed Commerce. Audio craft: denoise, level, transitions, stingers, so sponsors felt their brand was protected and guests felt their time was respected. Distribution: go-to-market strategy, captions and audiograms, and the Hivebrite Media Resource Center so community members could access episodes and resources.

Outcomes

30+ episodes with tech leaders at every level. 18% reach within the 8,000+ QueerTech professional network. The show continues beyond my tenure.

What this demonstrates. Podcast production is project management in audio form: stakeholder alignment, quality control, and a system that ships consistently. I built it once, handed it off, and it kept running.
